# Changelog

Versions are `Beta 0.MINOR.PATCH` until Town Hall 10 (`1.0.0`).

## Beta 0.6.1
- World map you can sail around: a larger chart of the north (Iceland to the Baltic, Norway to Frankia); drag or swipe to move, wheel or pinch to zoom, zoom and home buttons; mist at the edges and over lands not yet open.

## Beta 0.6.0
- PC interface: bottom dock (panels, build categories, roads), build tray with building pictures, inspector docked on the left, hover highlight and name.
- To do list of suggested actions that moves the camera or opens the right sheet.
- Resource bar grouped by kind, with daily change and a hover breakdown by producer.
- Map 132 × 132 with islands and a tarn, edge mist, softer locked land with a faint grid, smoother ground.
- Mountains as one low-poly massif with cliffs and snow.
- Roads on the ground, continuous bridges, props kept off roads and other plots.
- Per-building look (walls, roof, colours), six house colour schemes, new art for brewery, beehives, tailor, hof, watchtower and ship-tomb; Gallery in Test mode.
- Town Hall no longer requires winters survived.
- Limits and house capacity rebalanced: a full village has 4–8 spare workers and every warrior place filled.
- Fix: ship-tomb progress was lost on load. Old saves do not carry over.

## Beta 0.5.2
- Fix: the game froze when placing a house.

## Beta 0.5.1
- Road and palisade tool: the second click fixes the plan; Build road or Cancel. Build menu tabs wrap; top bar scrolls with the mouse.

## Beta 0.5.0
- Installable game (GitHub Pages, works offline). Town Hall 4–5 content: new buildings, troops, ships, regions, market with partners, palisade, banquets, mercenaries.
- Test mode. New houses and Town Hall plaza, feasts, raid animations, morale glow. Larger map. Balance tuned with bots.
