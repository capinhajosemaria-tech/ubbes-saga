# Changelog

Versions are `Beta 0.MINOR.PATCH` until Town Hall 10 (`1.0.0`).

## Beta 0.20.1
- "God" and "Gods" are now written with a capital letter in every text of the game.

## Beta 0.20.0
- **A new opening for every saga:** three illustrated story pages present the year 865, Ubbe and Kaldstrand over the new village. Then come three choice pages with picture cards: Ubbe's gift, the patron god (gift, blessing, offering and wrath on each card) and the first advisor.
- **New guide (11 steps)** for today's game: seasons, food and wood (with sources that run out), builders, worker priority, fish, planks, preserving, firewood, the Production panel and reserves, your patron god and the Feast, tools and the Town Hall. Each step has a picture, a task list that ticks itself, a button that does the step, and the button it is talking about pulses. On PC the guide sits on the left and moves aside when a sheet opens.
- **Gold in the top bar:** a gold counter appears as soon as you have any gold. Silver is shown from the start.
- **Clicking picks what is on the tile:** a click (and the hover glow) chooses the building on the tile under the cursor, then the tree, bush or ore on it. Only an empty tile falls back to a tall roof drawn over it. Trees, bushes and ore glow on hover too.

## Beta 0.19.0
- **New saga choices:** after the intro you choose Ubbe's trait (Builder, Warrior, Trader, Provider or Skald, bonuses only), the patron god and the first advisor.
- **One patron god:** only your patron (Odin, Thor, Freyja or Njord) has a favour bar. Each asks his own offering, made from the Town Hall or the Altar (+50% there): Odin preserved food, Thor iron, Freyja berries, Njord fish. Each keeps his gift at 50 and his blessing at 80 (asked at the Altar).
- **Patron's wrath:** below 25 favour (not in the first year) you are warned, and 10 days later the god strikes, each in his own way: plague, lightning, blight or storms.
- **The Feast** replaces the banquets and the three festivals: hold it from the Town Hall at any level; with ale or mead (Town Hall 3) it does more. The holy days (start of summer, start of winter, midwinter) are announced: a feast then pleases the patron twice as much.
- The council grows to 4 advisors: the 2nd at Town Hall 3, the 3rd at 4, the 4th at 5.
- **Trade map** (from the Market or the Trading House): the partner villages on the world chart. Answer their requests, or offer your goods for silver or for theirs; they accept, refuse or haggle. Each village has a relation that grows with good deals and gives better Market prices. The goods go by knarr and the payment comes home with it days later.
- **More newcomers when people are happy:** with free beds, morale above 55 brings 1.5× more people, 70 or more 2.2× (more couples too). The Attractiveness panel says so.
- **Berths:** each Harbour moors 2 ships, one on each side, and the Shipyard has its own pier for 1. When every berth is taken the Shipyard cannot start a new ship. At Town Hall 5 you can build a second Harbour. The sheets show berths used and free.
- **Rotate to all 4 sides:** buildings now turn four ways; on the last two turns the entrance is on the far side (out of sight), with the path and road behind.
- Older sagas still load: they get no trait, and the god with the most favour becomes the patron.

## Beta 0.18.1
- Sandbox mode is now truly free: endless stores (costs are free, the top bar shows ∞), every building and every upgrade up to Town Hall 5 open from the start, with Town Hall 5 limits.
- In Sandbox, construction, upgrades and the Town Hall finish at once, and the Town Hall needs no people or house level.
- In Sandbox all the land is unlocked from the start (also for older sandbox saves).
- People still arrive, work, eat and live as in the other modes.

## Beta 0.18.0
- Much more wood: woodcutters make 5 wood per worker a day (was 3), and a lodge needs only 20 trees of its own (was 45). Each tree gives 50 wood a year.
- Sources run out again: trees 50 wood, berry bushes 45, sea 100 fish a tile (river 70), iron outcrops 80. Used-up trees stand as stumps and grow back 3 days after winter; bushes and fish come back when summer starts; iron every two years, when winter starts.
- A building whose sources are all used up sends its workers to other jobs until they come back. Its sheet shows what is left and when it returns.
- New Market button in the bottom bar: buy and sell every good with each village that trades with you, at any time, in 1, 10 or 50. Each village has its own prices, which move with your deals and settle again over the days.
- The Trading House now opens the market and shows the visiting merchant's special orders (+50%).
- The silver in the top bar updates right after each deal, also while the game is paused.

## Beta 0.17.1
- Workers: instead of choosing a number per building, each workplace now has a priority: Stopped, Low, High or Urgent. Urgent jobs are filled completely first, then High, then Low share the rest. New buildings start at High.
- Berry gatherers stop by themselves in winter and go back to their own priority in summer.
- Builders now work from the Town Hall: 3 builder posts at Town Hall 1, one more per level, with their own priority on the Town Hall sheet. They only take people while something is being built.
- Every building sheet shows how many people work there; the hover tip and the People → Workplaces table show the number and the priority. An open building sheet updates every day.
- Fixed: free workers were not given back when you took people off a building, and the numbers on an open sheet did not update.

## Beta 0.17.0
- Music: 20 minutes of new pieces played with real recorded instruments: 4 summer pieces (harp, flute, fiddle, hand-drum grooves) and 4 winter pieces (low strings, horn, frame drum, wind), following the seasons.
- Special music when the fleet sails, when the ships come home, for a feast, a new Town Hall level and the death of a jarl.
- Music button under Home (on/off); volume and "now playing" in the Saga panel.
- Turning buildings now switches between their two pictures, and the door marker, path and night light match the drawn door on every building. Mill and Watchtower no longer show a Rotate button.
- The Shipyard is 3 × 3: boathouse on the shore (roads can touch it), quay and slipway over the sea, all inside its plot. Old shipyards move to the nearest spot that fits.

## Beta 0.16.1
- Every building casts a shadow in its own shape: walls, gable and hip roofs, towers, spires, sheds and booths, swinging with the sun.
- The light trodden ground under a building now hugs the building instead of filling its whole square plot (fenced pens, the market and the Town Hall plaza keep their square yard).
- At night the stray glowing "door" squares beside buildings are gone; the buildings' own doors and windows glow.

## Beta 0.16.0
- New art for every building, drawn in one style: fine lines, a real door on every building, stone footings, timber frames, glowing windows with shutters, and roofs of thatch, slate, black shingles or red boards.
- Houses are cottages that grow with each level, in 8 colour schemes (pick one in the house sheet, or keep a mixed village).
- New Town Hall (red-board hall with a carved portal and a feast hearth on the plaza), Jarl's House, Hof, Market, Harbour, Shipyard, Ship-Tomb and all workshops, pens, fields and decor.
- The Funeral Pyre only burns after a cremation.
- New grass: tall grass that sways in the wind, with a few wildflowers in summer.

## Beta 0.15.5
- A Woodcutter's Lodge now needs 45 trees of its own for full output (was 90).
- A Fisher's Hut needs 5 water tiles of its own for full output; each water tile serves one hut only.

## Beta 0.15.4
- Sources still never run out, but each tree, bush and iron outcrop now belongs to one building only. Full output needs 90 trees for a Woodcutter's Lodge, 6 bushes for a Berry Gatherer and 5 iron outcrops for a mine; with fewer, output falls in proportion. Placement and the building sheet show how many it has. Fishing water stays unlimited.

## Beta 0.15.3
- Trees, berry bushes, iron and fishing water never run out: one source in reach is enough for full output.
- Houses burn 30% less firewood (about 0.35 a day in summer, 0.84 in winter).

## Beta 0.15.2
- The small paths from building doors to the roads are gone.
- The palisade is now a closed wall of upright split pine logs, sharpened at the top and set in an earth bank (iron bands on the strongest level), and the gate has heavy log posts and a plank walkway. It no longer looks like a fence.

## Beta 0.15.1
- Every text in the game now uses the title font (Marcellus SC).
- Updates arrive at once: the game no longer keeps showing the previous version after a release.

## Beta 0.15.0 — Round 1C: production panel
- New **Production** panel in the bottom bar: for every good, what is in store, made and used each day, the net, and how many **winter days** it lasts (counting what stops in winter: fields, berries, bees and herds rest, fishing falls, firewood rises).
- **Reserves** for every good a workshop uses, in the same table. Workshops never go below them (now also wood for the smithy, smokehouse, bakery and brewery). New buttons: **Reserve for next Town Hall** (reserves its wood, planks and iron once) and **Clear reserves**.
- Click a good to see its **production chain**: the buildings that make it, with pictures, what one worker makes and arrows with the amounts, from raw goods to the finished one. Also "Made by" and "Used by", and what a winter day will look like.
- New **Upgrades** list in the bottom bar: every building that can be upgraded, affordable ones first, with a green count on the button; click one to fly to it.
- Build menu: the lock line stands out, and every card ends with its cost as large icons; goods you lack are red.
- Old saves still load.

## Beta 0.14.0 — Round 1: economy, resources and first art
- **Old saves do not load** (the economy changed too much).
- Trees, berry bushes, iron outcrops and fishing water now give a fixed amount each year (tree 8 wood, bush 25 berries, iron tile 60, sea tile 20 fish, river tile 12), refilled every spring. Buildings that share sources split them; placing shows how much a building will get, and its sheet shows the % of full output.
- Firewood: houses burn ½ wood a day in summer and 1.2 in winter. When logs run out, fires burn planks. Woodcutters keep full output in winter.
- Buildings take 1–3 days to build (and to upgrade) and borrow 1–3 free workers as builders; the plot shows scaffolding and progress.
- Town Hall: now needs people, materials and one house at the highest level your hall allows. No fame, building or saga-goal requirements. "Keep materials for the next hall" is gone.
- New **Remove** tool next to Roads: buildings, roads, trees and bushes. Building over trees or bushes asks first and gives back a little wood and berries.
- No warrior limit: the Training Ground trains free workers, limited by gear.
- Houses, granaries and warehouses have no limit (granaries and warehouses cost more after the second).
- New food chains: Pig Pen, Sheep Pen (wool), Fodder Field (animal feed), Grain Field → Water Mill (on the river) → Bakery (bread all year). Salt Works, Hunter's Lodge, Vegetable Garden and Hay Meadow are gone.
- Brewery and Beehives open at Town Hall 3. Ale and mead are no longer counted as meals: they are drunk at banquets and in the new **Tavern**, which raises morale.
- Art: "trodden earth" roads with door paths that join them, soft drifting grass, a new Market with timber booths and a well, the Water Mill with its wheel in the river, the Tavern, Pig and Sheep Pens, Fodder Field and construction scaffolding.
- New **Harbour**: a long pier from the shore out to sea with a boathouse, crane and lantern; ships moor alongside without overlapping. Karvi, knarr and snekkja now each look different.
- The guide explains yearly source limits, building time and the new Town Hall requirements.

## Beta 0.13.0

## Beta 0.12.0
- New look, "Frost & Ember": a warm, golden summer, cast shadows that move with the sun, and a slow time-of-day cycle (dawn, a long day, dusk and a short night) over every 3 game days, longer nights in winter. From dusk to dawn doors and fires glow.
- New map, the fjord: Kaldstrand lies at the head of a winding fjord with cliffs, a river from the northern range, a rocky iron hill next to the village, a ridge, a lake and islands at the fjord mouth.
- The starting land now always has iron close by (there was none before), plus berries, forest and water.
- Raiders only land from the open sea, never on the lake.
- Old saves do not carry over.

## Beta 0.11.0
- World map: names no longer overlap, Sweden is named and Birka sits on its island off the Swedish coast, England stays in sight under a light mist, and the map opens showing every place you can reach.
- Raids no longer starve the village: provisions not eaten come home as dried fish, and ships only sail if the village keeps enough food (the whole winter's when winter is near).
- When the warrior limit is full, weaker troops at home can retrain as vikings or berserks.
- Raiders on Kaldstrand carry black-and-yellow shields, so you can tell them from your own men.
- Village raids settled off screen take at most 20 goods per surviving raider, and a closed palisade now helps the defence (it wrongly helped the raiders).
- Market: iron and tools cost more to buy, and the price climbs when you buy a lot.
- Cowed villages pay half a plunder as tribute; Shetland and the Hebrides are closer and richer.

## Beta 0.10.0
- Raiders land at Kaldstrand from Town Hall 5: scouts warn you 5 days ahead, then you watch the fight. Raiders head for the Town Hall and hack through the palisade where they must; your warriors strike over the stakes, archers shoot from manned towers. Raiders who get in carry off goods to their ships. A report follows.
- The palisade has hit points and three levels (Stakes, Double stakes, Iron-bound stakes), upgraded in War → Defence. Damaged stakes are patched in a few days; broken segments must be repaired.
- Fix: houses no longer change shape or colour when moved, and all houses share one gable shape again (colour schemes kept). Every building checked in all four rotations.
- The Hof has a proper stave-church top: stacked steep roofs, gilded dragons and a weathervane.
- Build menu reorganised: Homes, Food, Farms, Materials, Crafts, Storage, Community, Military, Sea, Decor (no more two "Village" and two "War" buttons). Each card says what the building does and what it needs; building sheets explain it too.
- More detail close up: gravel and hoof prints on roads, grass and flowers, wood grain on walls, stones in footings, rope lashings on the palisade.
