# Changelog

Versions are `Beta 0.MINOR.PATCH` until Town Hall 10 (`1.0.0`).

## Beta 0.15.4
- Sources still never run out, but each tree, bush and iron outcrop now belongs to one building only. Full output needs 90 trees for a Woodcutter's Lodge, 6 bushes for a Berry Gatherer and 5 iron outcrops for a mine; with fewer, output falls in proportion. Placement and the building sheet show how many it has. Fishing water stays unlimited.

## Beta 0.15.3
- Trees, berry bushes, iron and fishing water never run out: one source in reach is enough for full output.
- Houses burn 30% less firewood (about 0.35 a day in summer, 0.84 in winter).

## Beta 0.15.2
- The small paths from building doors to the roads are gone.
- The palisade is now a closed wall of upright split pine logs, sharpened at the top and set in an earth bank (iron bands on the strongest level), and the gate has heavy log posts and a plank walkway. It no longer looks like a fence.

## Beta 0.15.1
- Every text in the game now uses the title font (Marcellus SC).
- Updates arrive at once: the game no longer keeps showing the previous version after a release.

## Beta 0.15.0 — Round 1C: production panel
- New **Production** panel in the bottom bar: for every good, what is in store, made and used each day, the net, and how many **winter days** it lasts (counting what stops in winter: fields, berries, bees and herds rest, fishing falls, firewood rises).
- **Reserves** for every good a workshop uses, in the same table. Workshops never go below them (now also wood for the smithy, smokehouse, bakery and brewery). New buttons: **Reserve for next Town Hall** (reserves its wood, planks and iron once) and **Clear reserves**.
- Click a good to see its **production chain**: the buildings that make it, with pictures, what one worker makes and arrows with the amounts, from raw goods to the finished one. Also "Made by" and "Used by", and what a winter day will look like.
- New **Upgrades** list in the bottom bar: every building that can be upgraded, affordable ones first, with a green count on the button; click one to fly to it.
- Build menu: the lock line stands out, and every card ends with its cost as large icons; goods you lack are red.
- Old saves still load.

## Beta 0.14.0 — Round 1: economy, resources and first art
- **Old saves do not load** (the economy changed too much).
- Trees, berry bushes, iron outcrops and fishing water now give a fixed amount each year (tree 8 wood, bush 25 berries, iron tile 60, sea tile 20 fish, river tile 12), refilled every spring. Buildings that share sources split them; placing shows how much a building will get, and its sheet shows the % of full output.
- Firewood: houses burn ½ wood a day in summer and 1.2 in winter. When logs run out, fires burn planks. Woodcutters keep full output in winter.
- Buildings take 1–3 days to build (and to upgrade) and borrow 1–3 free workers as builders; the plot shows scaffolding and progress.
- Town Hall: now needs people, materials and one house at the highest level your hall allows. No fame, building or saga-goal requirements. "Keep materials for the next hall" is gone.
- New **Remove** tool next to Roads: buildings, roads, trees and bushes. Building over trees or bushes asks first and gives back a little wood and berries.
- No warrior limit: the Training Ground trains free workers, limited by gear.
- Houses, granaries and warehouses have no limit (granaries and warehouses cost more after the second).
- New food chains: Pig Pen, Sheep Pen (wool), Fodder Field (animal feed), Grain Field → Water Mill (on the river) → Bakery (bread all year). Salt Works, Hunter's Lodge, Vegetable Garden and Hay Meadow are gone.
- Brewery and Beehives open at Town Hall 3. Ale and mead are no longer counted as meals: they are drunk at banquets and in the new **Tavern**, which raises morale.
- Art: "trodden earth" roads with door paths that join them, soft drifting grass, a new Market with timber booths and a well, the Water Mill with its wheel in the river, the Tavern, Pig and Sheep Pens, Fodder Field and construction scaffolding.
- New **Harbour**: a long pier from the shore out to sea with a boathouse, crane and lantern; ships moor alongside without overlapping. Karvi, knarr and snekkja now each look different.
- The guide explains yearly source limits, building time and the new Town Hall requirements.

## Beta 0.13.0
- The Town Hall now stands in the middle of its square, and you can move and rotate it (rotating turns the hall and moves the plaza fire to another corner).
- The Jarl's House is already built when a new saga starts.
- Locked buildings say what unlocks them, e.g. "Unlocks with: saga goal “Ready for winter”" or "Unlocks with: Town Hall 3".
- The building sheet shows output from workers as a bar and a percentage: 2 of 4 workers means 50%.
- The bottom bar has a gold border all round and its labels use the title font.
- Art: roads are earthier, with ragged grassy edges, ruts, puddles and edge stones; meadows, sand and building yards have more texture.
- Old saves still load (they keep their Town Hall where it was and get no free Jarl's House).

## Beta 0.12.0
- New look, "Frost & Ember": a warm, golden summer, cast shadows that move with the sun, and a slow time-of-day cycle (dawn, a long day, dusk and a short night) over every 3 game days, longer nights in winter. From dusk to dawn doors and fires glow.
- New map, the fjord: Kaldstrand lies at the head of a winding fjord with cliffs, a river from the northern range, a rocky iron hill next to the village, a ridge, a lake and islands at the fjord mouth.
- The starting land now always has iron close by (there was none before), plus berries, forest and water.
- Raiders only land from the open sea, never on the lake.
- Old saves do not carry over.

## Beta 0.11.0
- World map: names no longer overlap, Sweden is named and Birka sits on its island off the Swedish coast, England stays in sight under a light mist, and the map opens showing every place you can reach.
- Raids no longer starve the village: provisions not eaten come home as dried fish, and ships only sail if the village keeps enough food (the whole winter's when winter is near).
- When the warrior limit is full, weaker troops at home can retrain as vikings or berserks.
- Raiders on Kaldstrand carry black-and-yellow shields, so you can tell them from your own men.
- Village raids settled off screen take at most 20 goods per surviving raider, and a closed palisade now helps the defence (it wrongly helped the raiders).
- Market: iron and tools cost more to buy, and the price climbs when you buy a lot.
- Cowed villages pay half a plunder as tribute; Shetland and the Hebrides are closer and richer.

## Beta 0.10.0
- Raiders land at Kaldstrand from Town Hall 5: scouts warn you 5 days ahead, then you watch the fight. Raiders head for the Town Hall and hack through the palisade where they must; your warriors strike over the stakes, archers shoot from manned towers. Raiders who get in carry off goods to their ships. A report follows.
- The palisade has hit points and three levels (Stakes, Double stakes, Iron-bound stakes), upgraded in War → Defence. Damaged stakes are patched in a few days; broken segments must be repaired.
- Fix: houses no longer change shape or colour when moved, and all houses share one gable shape again (colour schemes kept). Every building checked in all four rotations.
- The Hof has a proper stave-church top: stacked steep roofs, gilded dragons and a weathervane.
- Build menu reorganised: Homes, Food, Farms, Materials, Crafts, Storage, Community, Military, Sea, Decor (no more two "Village" and two "War" buttons). Each card says what the building does and what it needs; building sheets explain it too.
- More detail close up: gravel and hoof prints on roads, grass and flowers, wood grain on walls, stones in footings, rope lashings on the palisade.

## Beta 0.9.1
- Fix: roofs of buildings facing the other way (for example after rotating them) were drawn crossed, so the ground showed through. They are solid again.

## Beta 0.9.0
- Town Hall 1 is quicker to get ready for winter: the Preserver unlocks with the first goal and the Granary and Warehouse with the planks goal. Iron and tools are now one goal, and the "4 houses" goal is gone (you start with 4).
- A short guide for new sagas: a card next to the To do list walks you through seasons, food, wood, workers, fish, planks, winter stores and firewood, and moves on as you build. Close it any time; reopen it from the Saga panel.
- Event choices now say clearly what you pay (red, −) and what you get (green, +), with icons. Festivals and the jarl's funeral too.
- Firewood: one more woodcutter's lodge allowed at Town Hall 3, 4 and 5; from Town Hall 3 newcomers wait before winter while firewood is short; a To do warning 10 days before winter when the wood will not last it.
- Roads stay joined where they run from grass down onto sand.
- New colour icons for the dock panels, build categories, roads and palisade; build costs shown with resource icons.
- The intro now says sixteen settlers, as in the game.

## Beta 0.8.0
- Water is gone: no more wells, water carriers or water stock — the village lives by the river. Fisher's Huts catch fully within 5 tiles of the sea or the river and less further away; the placement shows the catch.
- New buildings: the Town Hall is a great bowed longhouse with dragon heads that grows a raised roof from level 3, on a cobblestone plaza. Workshops and houses get their own shapes: round huts, open sheds, a granary on posts, a stone forge, hipped and bowed halls.
- Loose log piles removed; logs only at the Woodcutter's Lodge, with round ends.
- Classic menu look on PC with the bottom dock kept; building pictures in the build bar are now centred and sized to fit.
- Numbers in the title font; 6 × 6 land blocks outlined on locked land.
- Old saves do not carry over.

## Beta 0.7.0
- A bigger start: 16 people (a new couple with a child and two young adults) and 4 houses with 20 beds; newcomers arrive faster in the first year. Town Hall 2 now needs 22 people.
- More land from the start: 21 free zones (a 5 × 5 block minus its corners) instead of 9.
- New mountains: lower and narrower, grey ridged peaks with snow only on top; the spur north of the village pulled back, opening a wide meadow. Lighter mist and shading over rock.
- Resource bar: each resource shows its name, and new, more detailed icons.
- Fix: the To do list said "Every bed is taken" while beds were still free; it now says "Only 1 bed left" when one is free.
- Old saves do not carry over.
